## Requests

- Can't find a movie or show? **Request it** from Search, or from Settings > Requests, through your own [Seerr](https://github.com/seerr-team/seerr) server (Jellyseerr/Overseerr). Sign in with your Jellyfin login; only a session token is kept, never the password.
- Tap a result to see its details (poster, overview, cast, rating) before requesting. Shows ask which seasons.
- **My requests** has Pending, Approved and Denied tabs with counts.
- Posters for requests come from TMDB (`image.tmdb.org`); this is the only service besides your own servers that the app contacts, and only when requests are switched on.

## Admin (Jellyfin administrators only)

- A shield button next to the tab bar opens a dashboard: server name and version, movie/show/episode counts, a box per library with its own **scan** button, **Scan all**, **Restart server** (asks first), who is watching right now, and the pending Seerr requests with **Approve** and **Decline**.
- Other accounts never see it.

## Home

- **Favourites** card at the end of the Libraries row opens everything you marked as a favourite.
- **Recommended for you** row, from what the server suggests for you.
- Tap Home again to scroll back to the top.

## Player

- Playback speed up to 4x.
- Subtitle style options (bold, background, thick outline) now also apply to styled (ASS) subtitles. While one is on, typeset signs lose their own look.
- iPad: space pauses and the arrow keys skip 10 seconds; the iPad stays landscape after a video unless it is held upright.

## Downloads

- The Downloads tab shows the downloads of every account on the device, signed in or not, online or not. With more than one account, each row says who downloaded it.

## Fixes
