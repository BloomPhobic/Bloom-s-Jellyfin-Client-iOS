## iPad

- Runs natively on iPad (full screen): library and collection grids add columns to fill the width, show and details pages stay at a comfortable width, and posters and banners are larger.
- iPhone looks exactly as before.

## Shows and episodes

- Tapping an episode plays it straight away; the small **(i)** button opens its details.
- Select mode (checklist icon, or hold an episode and choose **Select**): tick several episodes, then mark them watched or unwatched, download them or remove the downloads. A short message says what happened.
- **Download next 3 unwatched** button on show pages.

## Languages

- Settings has a **Languages** page: ordered audio and subtitle wishes, one default list plus an optional list per library. "Original" is the file's main audio track; "Off" is no subtitles. If nothing on your list exists in a video, its own default is used.

## Accounts and profile

- Change your password and profile picture from Settings > Accounts, with a crop tool for the picture. The server decides what is allowed.

## Also

- The floating tab bar hides while you type, so it never covers the field.
- Downloads can be watched when you are signed out or offline.
- The Settings account card shows the current account, with other accounts below.

This is an unsigned build. Install `BloomsPlayer-1.3.0.ipa` with SideStore or LiveContainer, which sign it on your device.
