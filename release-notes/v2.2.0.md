## Watch later

- Add movies, shows and episodes to **Watch later** from their page, and find them on the Watch later card among your libraries. It is separate from Favourites.
- A title leaves the list once you finish it in the app or mark it watched; a show leaves once every episode is watched.

## Libraries

- **See all** next to Libraries on Home shows them in a grid. **Reorder** puts them in your own order, saved per account.

## Fixes

- Pages no longer open twice.
- The account button on Home works with the device held sideways, on iPhone and iPad.

This is an unsigned build. Install `BloomsPlayer-2.2.0.ipa` with SideStore or LiveContainer, which sign it on your device.
