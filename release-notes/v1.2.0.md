## Calmer player

- The info button now shows just the format, frame rate and how much is buffered. Everything else is still collected: **Copy all stats to log** writes it all to the log.
- Subtitle track and subtitle look are one panel with **Track** and **Style** tabs, replacing two separate buttons.

## Streaming and downloads

- Bigger read-ahead for streaming: up to 1 GB is kept on the phone (capped by free space), and the loaded part shows on the seek bar. Downloaded files don't use it.
- Long-press a download to mark it watched or unwatched. It works offline and the server is updated when you reconnect.
- Tapping the Downloads tab again takes you back to its main page.

## Also

- The "Not connected" page is redesigned, with a **Go to downloads** button.
- Search puts the best title match first, so an exact title is no longer buried under longer ones.
- Settings has a separate Playback page, and Diagnostics moved under About. The player shows a quality badge (1080p, 4K HDR) next to the title.
- If iOS refuses to rotate the screen when a video opens, the app tries again and writes details to the log.

This is an unsigned build. Install `BloomsPlayer-1.2.0.ipa` with SideStore or LiveContainer, which sign it on your device.
