## New: picture in picture

Swipe home while a video plays and it carries on in a small floating window. You can move it around, resize it, pause, skip back and forward, or tap the button in the window to go back to the full-screen player.

- Turn it off in **Settings → Picture in picture** if you'd rather playback stopped when you leave the app.
- Locking the phone while the window is open no longer leaves a black or frozen picture: the video comes back about a second after you unlock.

## Also

- The log in Settings no longer repeats the same player error hundreds of times, which makes it easier to share.

This is an unsigned build. Install `BloomsPlayer-1.1.0.ipa` with SideStore or LiveContainer, which sign it on your device.
