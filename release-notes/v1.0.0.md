The first release of Bloom's Player, a lightweight Jellyfin client for iPhone that plays your files directly through mpv, so the server never has to transcode.

This is an unsigned build. Install `BloomsPlayer-1.0.0.ipa` with SideStore or LiveContainer, which sign it on your device.

## What's in it

- **Direct play through mpv:** hardware decoding, HDR10 passthrough, ASS/PGS subtitles and external subtitle files, and a choice of audio and subtitle tracks
- **The player:** skip intro and credits, play the next episode automatically, chapters, preview pictures while scrubbing (when the server has made them), pinch to fill the screen, playback speed and hold-to-speed, and lock screen and Control Center controls
- **Servers with several addresses** (local network, Tailscale, a public domain): the app always uses the best one that answers, and switches when the network changes
- **Accounts:** several accounts, with sign-in through the server's user list, a password or Quick Connect
- **Browsing:** Home with Continue watching and Next up, library grids with filters, show and season pages, cast and "More like this", person pages, genres and search
- **Downloads:** original files saved in the background and played offline, with progress synced when you're back online

Development builds of every change are still published as the "Development build" pre-release.
