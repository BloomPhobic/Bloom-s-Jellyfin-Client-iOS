Privacy Policy

Bloom's Player, a client for Jellyfin · Last updated 13 October 2026

In short: the app collects nothing. It talks only to the Jellyfin server you add yourself. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to the developer.

What the app connects to

Bloom's Player connects only to the Jellyfin server addresses you enter. It sends your sign-in details to that server, and loads your libraries, artwork and videos from it. It also reports what you watch and your playback position back to that server, so your progress stays in sync with your other Jellyfin apps. The app uses no other online services.

Your Jellyfin server is run by you or by whoever invited you to it. What that server stores, such as watch history and logs, is up to its owner and is not covered by this policy.

What is stored on your device

  • Server addresses and the server's ID, so the app can reconnect.
  • Sign-in tokens for the accounts you add, kept in the iOS Keychain. Your password is sent to your server when you sign in, but it is never stored.
  • Your settings, such as the accent colour and subtitle and player options.
  • Cached artwork (posters and backdrops) to make browsing faster. You can clear it in Settings → Storage.
  • A diagnostics log that stays on the device. It only leaves the device if you copy it and share it yourself.

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else. Signing out removes that account's token. Deleting the app removes its data. iOS may keep Keychain items after the app is deleted, so sign out first if you want those removed too.

Permissions

  • Local Network: used only to reach a Jellyfin server on your home network.

The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or advertising identifier.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
